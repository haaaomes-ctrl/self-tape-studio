4. Branch Scope and Production Context
The Commercial branch covers TapeCoach’s operational commercial audition type and its use of the shared six-field model, especially the acting field as Commercial presence / naturalism. The completed Commercial refinement is intended to improve Commercial report specificity, field semantics, evidence anchoring, claim-scope control, self-tape assessability handling, professional presentation boundaries, accessibility-safe language and non-regression testing.
The revised Commercial outputs are proposed branch improvements. They are not yet shipped production behaviour unless a later implementation and rollout explicitly applies them.
Current live production behaviour remains separate from the research package. The live product continues to use the existing architecture, fields, weights, pipeline, schemas, scrubs and rendering system. This branch must not change those directly.
Commercial must preserve:
current six score fields;
Commercial weighting: acting as presence / naturalism 60%, brief_adherence 20%, technical 15%, audio 5%;
server-side recomputation;
caps, blockers, verdict thresholds and role-fit bounds;
Step 1 / Step 2 locked pipeline;
report schema and public JSON;
safety, material, presentation and accessibility scrubs;
Musical Theatre acting + song stabilised flow;
Dance, Acting and Voice / Singing label protections.
This branch must not convert marketability, bookability, “commercial look”, production polish, paid resources, social profile, charm, appearance or protected-characteristic assumptions into Commercial scoring evidence.

15. Compact Branch Handoff Pack
Commercial is mature enough for cross-branch consolidation. The branch is complete through source census, targeted extraction, synthesis, gap audit, revision, final audit, output-specificity mapping and implementation-readiness handoff. The stable ID families are COMMERCIAL-S001–S030, COMMERCIAL-B1-F01–F09, COMMERCIAL-B2-F01–F09, COMMERCIAL-B3-F01–F10, COMMERCIAL-B4-F01–F06, COMMERCIAL-SYN-F01–F24, COMMERCIAL-AUDIT-I01–I17, COMMERCIAL-AUDIT-R01–R21, COMMERCIAL-REV-C01–C21, COMMERCIAL-REV-S01–S21, COMMERCIAL-FINAL-D01–D02, COMMERCIAL-OS-R01–R21, COMMERCIAL-OS-T01–T31 and COMMERCIAL-OS-I01–I15.
The strongest evidence base comes from B1, B2 and B3. B1 establishes Commercial as distinct from theatre acting and generic screen acting, with evidence around grounded reality, task-calibrated tone, copy handling and camera relationship. B2 establishes assessability and anti-polish: Commercial feedback is only reliable when the tape is readable, audible and task-appropriate, and simple domestic capture can be acceptable. B3 establishes the official process, fairness, safety and access spine: reader access, deadlines, upload burden, paid resources, nudity/stunt/privacy/consent, protected characteristics and accessibility must not become talent evidence. B4 is conditional only, mainly supporting narrow voiceover-style copy analysis.
COMMERCIAL-AUDIT found the baseline directionally correct but too generic. COMMERCIAL-REV addressed this through wording and guardrail revisions only, preserving all score fields, weights, caps, blockers, verdict thresholds, role-fit bounds, schema, backend, pipeline, report schema and the MT acting + song anchor. COMMERCIAL-FINAL-AUDIT approved the revision with explicit limitations. COMMERCIAL-OUTPUT-SPEC created COMMERCIAL-OS-R01–R21 and COMMERCIAL-OS-T01–T31.
Commercial is implementation-ready as a production-facing requirement package, not as shipped production behaviour. The key implementation-readiness implications are: clarify acting as Commercial presence/naturalism through observable copy, camera, tone and product/situation evidence; block generic praise; prevent no-brief brand/product/audience invention; separate direct-to-camera from reader-scene work; treat assessability as a precondition; block polish/resource/marketability/look/bookability evidence; and require access-safe, non-deficit language.
Not verified: live Commercial output quality, frontend labels, comparison labels, renderer/export timestamp parity, persisted-versus-rendered timestamps and score stability. No Commercial branch patch is required before consolidation, but live Commercial examples are required before product release sign-off.
