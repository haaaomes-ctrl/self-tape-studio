17. Reusable Handoff Pack for COMMERCIAL-REV
COMMERCIAL-AUDIT handoff summary
COMMERCIAL-AUDIT is complete. The current Commercial baseline is directionally correct because Commercial is already an operational type and the supplied baseline recognises clarity, naturalism, camera connection, copy/brand tone and technical readability. However, COMMERCIAL-SYN shows that the baseline is still too generic to be revision-ready. It does not yet strongly prevent Commercial feedback from becoming theatre-style acting feedback or generic screen acting feedback, and it does not yet provide enough observable anchors for the acting field when it is used as Commercial presence / naturalism.
The highest-priority revision need is Commercial field semantics. COMMERCIAL-REV should clarify that Commercial acting/presence means observable copy communication, grounded task-calibrated tone, camera relationship, addressee logic, product/situation grounding where supplied and believable scale. Generic language such as “natural”, “confident”, “strong presence”, “relatable”, “authentic”, “marketable” or “bookable” must be replaced with specific behavioural evidence.
The second priority is claim scope. Commercial reports must not invent brand, product, buyer, target audience, campaign world or casting fit where no brief/copy/product context exists. Direct-to-camera, off-camera-reader and scene-style Commercial work need separate camera and eyeline handling. Voiceover-style evidence is conditional only. UGC/social, presenter-led product and corporate/industrial remain limitations.
The third priority is fairness and assessability. B2 supports clear sight/sound, task-appropriate framing, controlled eyeline and simple domestic capture as assessability conditions, while B3 blocks production polish, expensive equipment, paid readers, upload/fee burden, protected-characteristic inference, nudity/stunt/privacy overreach and access needs as deficits. Technical or audio weaknesses should narrow reliability before becoming Commercial-performance criticism.
No score fields, weights, caps, blockers, verdict thresholds, role-fit bounds, backend logic, schema, pipeline or UI changes are supported. COMMERCIAL-REV should create wording, evidence-standard and guardrail revisions only.
