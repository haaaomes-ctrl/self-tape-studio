# Voice / singing-voice rubric research — discipline overview

> **Discipline thread overview** (part of [[drr-programme-overview]]). 12-step builds-on chain, originals dated **5–6 May 2026**, imported 2026-06-08. The **full consolidated Voice rubric** is the archived *Merged* file `knowledge/90-archive/discipline-rubric-research/4.Voice/Voice Rubric Research Merged.md` (not embedded here, by design).
>
> **Taxonomy note (operator decision).** This thread is filed under `discipline: commercial` with a `voice` sub-area tag. That is an operator taxonomy choice for the corpus, **not** a conclusion of the research: in the source files "Voice" means the **sung / singing voice** (the Song / MT vocal reference), and the only asserted Voice↔Commercial relationship is a label-boundary. ## Arc

Voice (internally the "Voice / Singing" branch) was the **fourth** discipline and ran the full lean-v5 sequence: a ~46-candidate source census across four families → four extraction batches → synthesis → gap audit → revision → final audit → output-spec → V5 maturity audit → handoff. It solved how to assess **sung** performance without inventing technique-only or marketability claims, while protecting the shared `vocal` field.

## What the consolidated rubric covers

- An **observable vocal-descriptor spine**: pitch/intonation, rhythm/timing/tempo, tone/resonance, breath management (cautiously), diction/intelligibility, phrasing/shape, communication, interpretation, character/style and presence — each needing a specific tape-observable anchor, not "secure pitch" / generic praise.
- Song as **vocal-plus-interpretive** (acting-through-song: technique fused with lyric, story, character, objective and arc), kept song-framed and barred from leaking into spoken-scene concepts (reader, eyeline, scene partner).
- Style/subtype handling, **explicitly uneven**: strong for classical/legit, contemporary MT, art song, accompanied/unaccompanied; thin (carried as limitations) for belt, mix, registration, jazz, folk, commercial-pop and actor-musician.
- Assessability/audio separated from vocal merit (balance, framing, clipping, backing-track issues reduce *reliability*, not "weak singing").
- Claim-scope boundaries: no inferring live-room capacities (stamina, direction-response, learning speed) from a finished tape; external marks/grades/exam bands never become weights.
- Safety/fairness rails: no-diagnosis vocal-health observation only; access/neurodivergence/accent/gender-diverse voice treated as fairness context, not deficits.

## Deviations / findings

- **No "Lessons" step** — the chain runs straight from output-spec → V5 maturity audit → handoff (cross-branch lessons are consumed as input, not produced here).
- **`vocal`-field overload is the lead finding** (synthesis F01): `vocal` is reused across Song/MT (sung voice), Acting (speech-delivery) and Dance (movement-technique proxy). Voice's job was to *clarify, not rename* — rule OS-R01 fixes `vocal` = sung-vocal evidence in Song/MT only, preserving the Acting/Dance proxy meanings so singing labels never attach to speech or movement.
- **What "Voice" covers / the Commercial relationship:** here "Voice" = the **sung/singing voice** (Song/MT reference — admissions songs, cuts, legit vs contemporary, art song, accompanied/unaccompanied), **not** voiceover or commercial-VO. The files do *not* fold Voice under Commercial; they mandate a *label-boundary* (Voice's acting-through-song semantics must not leak into the Commercial branch's acting-as-presence/naturalism, and "commercial" must never mean marketability). See [[drr-voice-commercial-ratification-gap]].
- Passed all V5 gates with **no patch required**; the only standing gap is the absence of true Voice-only live outputs (display labels, comparison/PDF parity, timestamp rendering unverified).

## Key lesson

Disambiguate the overloaded `vocal` field at the rule layer (sung-vocal only in Song/MT) without renaming fields or adding schema — and anchor every vocal claim to specific observable evidence — so singing assessment never leaks into speech / Commercial / Dance and never drifts into technique-only praise, marketability or medical diagnosis.
