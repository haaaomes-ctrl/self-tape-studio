17. Reusable Handoff Pack for COMMERCIAL-AUDIT
COMMERCIAL-SYN handoff summary
COMMERCIAL-SYN is complete and establishes a source-grounded evidence base for auditing the current Commercial baseline. The strongest finding is that Commercial should not be treated as theatre acting or as generic screen acting. The B1 evidence supports Commercial as a distinct performance context centred on believable, relatable and grounded reality; task-calibrated tone; copy handling; and camera relationship. Direct-to-camera and off-camera-reader setups need different evidence expectations, and no-dialogue / silent-vignette Commercial work is a real but thinner sub-context.
The B2 evidence supplies the self-tape assessability layer. Finished-tape Commercial feedback is only trustworthy when the performer can be clearly seen and heard, framing is task-appropriate, eyeline is controlled, the reader setup is legible, and the tape is continuous enough to judge the work. This batch strongly supports a separation between technical assessability and Commercial performance quality. Domestic capture, phones and simple setups can be acceptable; expensive equipment, studio polish, editing and professional packaging must not become Commercial merit.

It adds useful but narrow support for voiceover-style copy analysis through product cues, visual cues, cue words and point of view, but it does not solve UGC/social-style, presenter-led product or corporate/industrial Commercial subtypes. It should not outrank B1, B2 or B3.
