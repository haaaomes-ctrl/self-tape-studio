## Reusable handoff and completion

**DANCE-OUTPUT-SPEC handoff summary**

The Dance output-specificity mapping is complete and the revised baseline is now translated into a concrete QA-ready standard. The strongest result is that the revised Dance rules can now be tested as output behaviour rather than treated as abstract rubric wording. In practical terms, Dance outputs must now behave as if assessability comes first. Full-body or task-sufficient visibility, stable framing, readable space, audible music where relevant, continuity and simple capture are all treated as preconditions for high-confidence comment. Where those conditions do not hold, the output must narrow its claim, state the limitation and reduce reliability instead of substituting generic criticism.

The second major result is that user-facing Dance wording is now tightly bounded. Dance-only outputs must not present the stored vocal slot as singing or voice where singing is absent. Movement technique must be described through style-aware or shared movement domains, not through one generic movement bucket and not through singing language. Performance language is now also constrained: Dance outputs should talk about phrasing, dynamics, focus, spatial intention, musical relation and choreographic communication, not generic acting unless spoken acting genuinely exists.

The third major result is testability. The accepted revision package now maps cleanly into preserved QA clusters: assessability and anti-polish boundaries; dance-only label containment; style specificity and generic-feedback suppression; live-room-only overclaim controls; accessibility-safe wording; commercial/street caution; tap audibility; and context-specific handling of improvisation and training-potential language. Because no live Dance reports were supplied, this handoff remains synthetic and adversarial rather than renderer-verified. That is the main remaining limitation.

Nothing in this mapping destabilises the shared six-field architecture, the two-step pipeline, or the Musical Theatre acting-plus-song anchor. The open issues are therefore limitations to carry into live-output QA, not blockers to the next stage.
