4. Branch Scope and Production Context
The Dance branch covers Dance audition assessment inside TapeCoach’s existing shared six-field architecture. It improves Dance-specific report behaviour by adding style-aware movement language, evidence anchors, assessability preconditions, live-room-only claim boundaries, anti-bias protections and user-facing label containment for the overloaded vocal field.
The revised Dance branch is intended to improve:
credibility of Dance reports;
movement-technique specificity;
full-body/task-sufficient visibility handling;
music/audio assessability handling;
generic-feedback suppression;
accessibility-safe and adapted-movement language;
anti-polish and anti-marketability boundaries;
timestamp density and usefulness;
user-facing category-label semantics.
Current live production remains separate from this completed branch work. The revised Dance requirements are not shipped production behaviour unless implementation and rollout have occurred. No live Dance-only output has been supplied or verified.
The Dance branch must not change:
shared score fields;
Dance weights;
backend/pipeline/data flow;
report schema or public JSON;
caps, blockers, thresholds or role-fit bounds;
MT acting + song stabilised flow;
completed Acting, Voice/Singing and Commercial semantics.

The key synthesis conclusion is that Dance cannot be treated as one generic movement standard. Dance outputs must be style-specific where justified, cautious where style is mixed or unclear, and grounded in observable movement evidence.
The accepted revision improves Dance at wording and guardrail level without changing the shared architecture. It contains the vocal proxy risk by requiring Dance-only user-facing labels to mean movement/dance technique, not singing or voice. It codifies assessability preconditions, blocks generic praise, ring-fences live-room-only claims, strengthens accessibility-safe assessment, constrains professional_presentation to assessability/brief response/safe preparation, and adds timestamp/reliability expectations.
The final audit approved Dance with explicit limitations. The branch is not yet production-verified because no Dance-only live reports, PDFs, JSONs or comparison examples exist. The uploaded outputs are Musical Theatre examples and cannot verify Dance behaviour. The pre-production QA plan therefore requires synthetic Dance outputs before release, with P0 tests for label containment, home setup, polish bias, cropped visibility, weak audio, tap audibility, adapted movement, pickup/stamina overclaim and unknown-style fallback.
No further Dance research or rubric rewrite is required before cross-branch consolidation. Product release should wait for pre-production synthetic QA and later first-output monitoring.
