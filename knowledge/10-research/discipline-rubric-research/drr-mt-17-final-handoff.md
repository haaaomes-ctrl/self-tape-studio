4. Branch Scope and Production Context
The Musical Theatre branch covers self-tape evaluation where acting and song are present, usually as a multi-component audition containing an acting scene and a song. It also covers MT-specific report behaviour around acting-through-song, vocal technique in service of story, scene-to-song integration, movement in service of story where visible, style/subtype language where observable, and fairness/access constraints.
The completed revised MT outputs are intended to improve report specificity inside the existing TapeCoach architecture. They are not intended to change the scoring model, weights, score fields, audition types, caps, blockers, verdict thresholds, role-fit bounds, report schema, backend pipeline or UI mechanisms.
Current live production behaviour remains separate. The four supplied current MT outputs preserve the core Acting + Song structure, but they do not yet satisfy the v5 output-specificity standard. The revision and output-specificity patch are requirements for future implementation planning; they are not evidence that production has already changed.
The branch must preserve the live system’s six score fields, current MT weights, acting + song detection, component breakdown, visible Acting and Vocal categories, consistency_modifier, timestamp coverage across acting and song, UK terminology, maximum 3 strengths, maximum 3 improvements and maximum 8 timestamped notes.
