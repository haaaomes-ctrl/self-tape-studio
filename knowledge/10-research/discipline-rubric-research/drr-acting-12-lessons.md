Recommended next discipline: Voice / Singing
Select Voice / Singing as the next discipline branch.
It is the highest-leverage next branch because the vocal field is the most overloaded shared field in the current TapeCoach architecture: it means sung vocal for Song/MT, speech delivery for Acting/Monologue, and movement-technique proxy for Dance. That creates label, weighting and report-interpretation risk across every completed branch.
Voice/Singing is also explicitly underdeveloped in the current baseline. The existing control sheet says Song/Voice already covers vocal performance, musical interpretation, acting/storytelling through song, audio clarity and brief adherence, but still lacks style-specific criteria for classical/legit, contemporary MT, belt, mix, pop/rock, jazz and folk, plus vocal-health/sustainability language, diction, resonance, passaggio, support, vibrato and stylistic authenticity.
This also protects the MT anchor. Musical Theatre is currently the stabilised flow and must continue to show both Acting and Vocal where acting and singing are present; the current baseline also notes that MT acting-through-song is still implicit and that song feedback can over-focus on vocal technique rather than lyric/storytelling.
Recommended branch order
Priority
Discipline branch
Why
1
Voice / Singing
Highest shared-field impact; closes the true sung-vocal meaning of vocal; supports Song and MT without reopening MT.
2
Commercial / Screen / Copy
Commercial is operationally present but subtype-light; acting now covers some screen/self-tape risks, so Commercial can build on that.
3
Additional Audition Types
Recall, workshop, actor-musician, presenting, voiceover, skills auditions and accent/dialect-only are not first-class types, so they need review after core disciplines are stable.
Why not Commercial next?
Commercial is important, but the current system already routes Commercial mainly through acting-as-presence/naturalism, brief, technical and audio. Its gap is subtype precision: to-camera, naturalistic screen, corporate/industrial and voiceover-style delivery. That is worth doing, but it is less foundational than cleaning up the true singing branch, because Voice/Singing affects Song, MT, label semantics, score interpretation and acting-through-song.

Use Deep Research only after preflight, when running the actual Voice/Singing source extraction.
