The singing-audition page supports diction clarity, open-eyed communication, addressee, lyric speaking for emotional levels, hope and freshness of moment. It can later support interpretive song language and anti-overproduction boundaries. It cannot support universal repertoire rules, belt/mix criteria or fixed scoring because it is practitioner/interview-shaped. citeturn34view4turn21view1turn21view3turn21view6

VOICE-B3 extracted 11 assigned source rows across official performer-platform guidance, union fair-process guidance, professional/practitioner advice, official company casting pages, an official theatre open-call set and one singer-specific professional organisation source.
