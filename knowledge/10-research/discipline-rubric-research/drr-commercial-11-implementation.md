3. Scope and Non-Implementation Boundary
This handoff covers the Commercial branch’s approved production-facing requirements: prompt/report behaviour expectations, output rules, guardrails, display-verification needs, live-QA scenarios, non-regression protections and residual limitations.
It does not cover implementation mechanism, code, schema edits, backend changes, UI design, prompt edits, data-flow changes, score recalculation, scoring-field changes, weighting changes, caps, blockers, verdict thresholds or role-fit bounds.
Actual implementation planning must decide how to apply the approved Commercial requirements inside production prompts, report behaviour, validation, display labels and QA workflows. Live-output QA must wait until implemented revised Commercial outputs or current relevant Commercial reports are available.
The following must remain preserved: the six-field model, Commercial weights, server-side recomputation, Step 1 / Step 2 locked pipeline, report schema, public JSON structure, safety/material/accessibility scrubs, UK terminology, timestamp limits and the Musical Theatre acting + song stabilised anchor.

4. Commercial Production-Readiness Summary
Commercial production-facing improvements should clarify that the acting field means Commercial presence / naturalism only when grounded in observable Commercial behaviour: copy communication, task-calibrated tone, camera relationship, addressee logic, believable scale, product / brand / situation context where supplied, and evidence visible in the tape.
Commercial must not be treated as theatre acting or generic screen acting. Direct-to-camera, off-camera-reader / scene-style, no-dialogue and voiceover-style contexts require distinct handling. UGC/social-style, presenter-led product and corporate/industrial contexts remain limitations unless the task or brief supplies enough context.
Commercial outputs must block no-brief invention: no unsupported brand, product, buyer, target audience, campaign world, casting fit or “commercial look” claims. Technical and audio evidence should support assessability and reliability; poor sight, sound or framing should narrow confidence before becoming performance criticism. Domestic capture and simple setups can be acceptable; production polish, expensive equipment, paid readers, paid editing or studio quality must not be treated as Commercial merit.
Reader access, upload burden, file admin, deadlines, slate details and turnaround are process-only unless they affect explicit brief compliance. Live-room capacities such as direction response, callback readiness and campaign-pressure flexibility must not be inferred from a finished tape.
Accessibility-safe Commercial handling must treat access needs, disability, visual impairment, Deaf/disabled access, hearing difference, speech difference, accent, gender-diverse voice, mobility difference, convalescence and adaptation as fairness / process / assessability contexts, not deficits.

The production-facing Commercial requirement is to make the existing Commercial type more specific inside the current six-field architecture. The acting field remains unchanged but should be interpreted in Commercial as observable presence / naturalism: copy communication, grounded task-calibrated tone, camera relationship, addressee logic, believable scale and product / brand / situation grounding where supplied. Commercial must not become theatre acting, generic screen acting, voiceover, UGC or presenting by default.
Commercial reports must require behavioural anchors in headline, category notes, strengths, improvements, fix-first, timestamps and next-take advice. Generic phrases such as “natural”, “confident”, “strong presence”, “relatable”, “authentic”, “marketable”, “bookable”, “commercial look” or “good camera work” must be replaced by observable evidence. Tone, copy, product, brand and audience claims require supplied or observable context. No-brief reports must not invent brand, product, buyer, target audience, campaign world or casting fit.
The main guardrails are assessability before judgement, simple capture over polish, reader/admin/deadline/process-only boundaries, no live-room overclaims, accessibility-safe non-deficit language and anti-marketability / anti-appearance exclusions. Technical and audio issues should narrow reliability before becoming Commercial-performance criticism. professional_presentation must stay limited to assessability, explicit brief compliance and safe process.
Display verification remains essential after implementation: category labels, acting-as-presence semantics, omission of irrelevant Vocal labels, comparison-page parity, timestamp count/order/specificity, feedback reliability and professional_presentation wording must be checked. No live Commercial outputs were supplied, so live Commercial QA remains pending. Required regression tests are COMMERCIAL-OS-T01–T31.
