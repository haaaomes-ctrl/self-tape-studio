**DANCE-B2 handoff summary**  
DANCE-B2 is complete at extraction level and materially strengthens the Dance branch in three areas. First, it gives the branch a formal descriptor base from exam-board sources, especially RAD and ISTD. RAD’s strongest contribution is a reusable domain set around posture/weight placement, coordination, control, line, spatial awareness, timing, responsiveness to music, expression, projection, interpretation and communication. ISTD then adds technique-specific depth: contemporary contributes breath, weight, alignment, flow, space and structured improvisation; modern theatre/jazz-adjacent material contributes musicality, projection, style/theme, line, use of space and presentational skills; tap contributes clarity of beating, rhythmic interpretation, footwork precision and audible response. citeturn20view1turn23view0turn13view1turn6view9

Second, B2 improves the fairness baseline. RAD and ISTD both make clear that reasonable adjustments should remove barriers without changing standards or giving unfair advantage, and that candidates remain assessed against the required attainment. This is important for later audit/revision because it supports accessibility-safe assessment and helps separate legitimate process adaptation from unlawful or unsafe inference about disability, injury, stamina or non-standard presentation. citeturn9view0turn17view2turn16view5

Third, B2 narrows the style gap for street/hip-hop and competition language, but mostly through competition frameworks rather than admissions. HHI is especially valuable for head-to-toe visibility, synchronization, musical phrasing, authenticity of style and full-body choreography. IDO helps show that jazz, show, tap and hip-hop are adjudicated through genuinely different systems. Prix, YAGP and U.DANCE add useful comparator evidence for video-selection contents, category structure, costume non-penalisation and stage-vs-screen distinctions, but these remain competition or platform specific rather than direct audition standards. citeturn16view9turn16view8turn16view6turn17view4turn18view3
