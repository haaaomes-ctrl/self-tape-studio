# Acting rubric research — discipline overview

> **Discipline thread overview** (part of [[drr-programme-overview]]). 14-step builds-on chain, originals dated **4–6 May 2026**, imported 2026-06-08. The **full consolidated Acting rubric** is the archived *Merged* file `knowledge/90-archive/discipline-rubric-research/3. Acting/Acting Rubric Research Merged.md` (not embedded here, by design).

## Arc

Acting was the **third** discipline through the now-standardised pipeline (census → four extraction batches across ~43 sources → synthesis → synthesis audit → revision → final audit → output-specificity mapping → MT acting+song bridge QA → lessons → V5 maturity audit → consolidation-ready handoff). It solved the problem that "acting" was being treated as one undifferentiated standard inside a shared six-field schema, replacing generic praise and a mislabelled `vocal` field with form-specific, evidence-anchored, tape-scope-bounded feedback rules.

## What the consolidated rubric covers

- Acting-form specificity: distinct evidence expectations for monologue, scene/duologue, screen/self-tape, stage, and classical/heightened vs contemporary text.
- Truthfulness / specificity of choices: every substantive claim needs a named behavioural anchor (beat change, tactic, stakes, text handling), not affect labels.
- Voice / speech delivery: clarity, diction, audibility, modulation, natural voice — framed as **acting** evidence, never singing.
- Screen / self-tape camera handling: task-specific eyeline / frame / direct-vs-off-lens; reader as process support; no universal eyeline rule.
- Assessability vs quality: readability/audio govern feedback *reliability*, not acting merit; production polish excluded.
- Anti-generic + scope rules: ban "grounded/natural/believable/connected" unless evidenced; ring-fence redirection/training-potential as live-room-only; block no-brief role/world invention; methodology-neutral.

## Deviations / findings

- The **`vocal`-as-speech overload** is the headline risk — contained at wording/display level only, since renaming the shared field was out of scope, leaving a residual schema-shaped limitation.
- No true Acting-only live-output QA exists (only the MT bridge QA), so label containment, comparison-page parity and renderer/export timestamp behaviour stay unverified.
- **Two files are mislabelled vs their contents** (preserved verbatim, titles kept stage-based): [[drr-acting-12-lessons]] (`A-Lessons Learned.md`) actually carries the next-discipline (Voice/Singing) recommendation, and [[drr-acting-10-output-spec]] (`A-Syn-OUTPUT-SPEC.md`) is a generic handoff template — the real Acting output-spec (OS-R01–R19) lives in §9 of [[drr-acting-14-final-handoff]].

## Key lesson

Generic acting praise without an observable behavioural anchor is the core failure mode; output-specificity (enforced, testable replacement of vague terms) — not improved rubric wording — is the acceptance standard.
