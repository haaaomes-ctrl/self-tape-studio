## Risks, exclusions and evidence gaps

**Generic feedback risks**

| Generic feedback risk | Why it is generic in MT | What observable evidence must replace it | Source basis | Example of the evidence the report must reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| “Great stage presence” | Can mean charm, charisma, type, volume, or simple confidence; it is too vague and bias-prone | What the performer actually did to hold focus: lyric ownership, target, movement clarity, phrase control | [MT-S065], [MT-S058], MT-B1-023 | A specific lyric or transition where attention was held because intention stayed precise |
| “Strong voice” | Says nothing about style, story, diction, or how voice served the material | Register event, style handling, diction, line, dynamic or phrase choice | [MT-S007], [MT-S072], MT-B1-020 | A belt/mix moment or Golden Age phrase that clarified text and style |
