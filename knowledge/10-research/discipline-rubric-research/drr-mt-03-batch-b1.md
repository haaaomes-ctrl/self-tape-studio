## Revised Reusable Handoff Pack for MT-B2

### MT-B1 completion summary

MT-B1 is now complete. The prior gap concerned four queued rows: Italia Conti CertHE Musical Theatre, Italia Conti FdA Musical Theatre, Royal Academy of Music Musical Theatre Audition Requirements, and the Royal Academy of Music Musical Theatre department page. All four have now been re-found, verified, and extracted.

The most important change is that **Italia Conti is no longer under-extracted**. Its current public CertHE and FdA pages now give explicit first-round and recall structures: contemporary British speech, song showing vocal and performance ability, set technical dance plus preferred-style solo, on-site recall, Golden Age versus post-1965 song contrast, recall dance classes, and interview. They also publish unusually useful signals around **acting while singing**, **voice/casting-type suitability**, **response to direction**, **dance assessability**, **reasonable adjustments**, and **audition-based level fit / non-standard entry**.

**Royal Academy of Music is also now complete and confirmed as genuine in MT-B1**, not a mistaken substitute for RADA. The audition-requirements page is valuable because it publishes a distinctive postgraduate video portfolio structure: three songs showing different voice qualities, vocal-range capture, a contemporary naturalistic monologue, a spontaneous motivation video, an unscripted personal-response clip, slate, and embedded self-tape advice. The department page remains a **context source**, not a public scoring rubric, but it is relevant for individuality, diversity, current-practitioner training, and industry-facing readiness.

The main MT-B1 evidence base is therefore broader and cleaner than before. The strongest official-institution sources remain Mountview’s digital handbook, Bird’s applications page, ArtsEd auditions, RWCMD guidance, RCS, Boston Conservatory, CCM best practices, NYU Tisch, Penn State, and now the completed Italia Conti and RAM rows. MT-B2 should not repeat this institutional work. It should now target the remaining public gap: **formal descriptor language, mark bands, adjudication vocabulary, and exam-board grading logic** for acting-through-song, vocal communication versus technique, movement assessment, and interpretation.

### Strongest official-institution findings to carry forward

- **Mountview** — explicit acting-through-song, legit versus contemporary contrast, full-body dance assessability, and recall expansion.
- **Bird** — unusually clear “current and potential skills” plus interview qualities: motivation, communication, flexibility.
- **ArtsEd** — one of the clearest public dance-room criteria sources: how you learn, listen, respond to instruction, and act through dance.
- **RWCMD** — strongest public values language around inhabiting character, risk, generosity, and fair technical consideration.
- **Italia Conti** — now a strong official source for Golden Age versus post-1965 contrast, acting-as-singing, direction-response, dance visibility, interview, and audition-based level fit.
- **Royal Academy of Music** — useful PG comparator for voice-quality contrast, vocal-range capture, naturalistic monologue, motivation/personhood-to-camera, and self-tape standards.
- **CCM** — clearest public vocabulary for vocal ability, physical awareness, communication skills, personality, and development of style.
- **NYU Tisch** — strongest integrated acting / singing / dance / conversation model.
- **Penn State** — explicit distinction between “professional potential” and “perfect casting decision”.

### MT-B2 gaps to resolve through exam boards / syllabi / adjudication

MT-B2 should now target the areas that institutional admissions pages still leave thin:

- formal **mark bands / descriptor language**
- explicit adjudication wording for **acting-through-song**
- clearer separation of **technical vocal execution** from **communication / storytelling**
- more formal grading language for **dance / movement assessment**
- exam-board handling of **individuality / interpretation / professionalism**
- any public descriptor language that can anchor later score calibration without inventing new rubric fields
