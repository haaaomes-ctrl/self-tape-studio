2. Source Census Scope
This source census covers public sources likely to support the Voice / Singing branch in four evidence families:
VOICE-B1 — official admissions / schools / conservatoires / MT programmes Real audition requirements, song tasks, accompaniment rules, cuts, self-tape requirements, acting-through-song, style expectations and live-room boundaries.
VOICE-B2 — formal frameworks / exam boards / vocal descriptor sources Vocal technique, musicality, pitch, timing, tone, diction, communication, style and musical interpretation descriptors.
VOICE-B3 — professional casting / self-tape / recorded-audio process guidance Audio/visual assessability, backing-track balance, self-tape burden, platform/process constraints, reader/accompanist/resource equity.
VOICE-B4 — accessibility / fairness / vocal-health-safety / gap-fill sources No-diagnosis rules, vocal strain/overload caution, speech/accent/access fairness, gender-diverse voice handling, disability/non-deficit overlays.
This census does not reopen Musical Theatre, Dance or Acting research, but it preserves their constraints: MT Vocal must remain visible where singing exists; Acting spoken-only speech must not be labelled as singing; Dance-only reports must not imply singing where none exists.

Medical advice, treatment plans, diagnostic claims, identity inference, body/appearance/voice-type assumptions, one pedagogy as default.
Completion criteria
Every B4 source marked as safety, accessibility, pedagogy-bounded, process-only or optional.
Likely limitations
Some sources are clinical or pedagogical, not audition-rubric sources; they should shape safety language, not scoring.
