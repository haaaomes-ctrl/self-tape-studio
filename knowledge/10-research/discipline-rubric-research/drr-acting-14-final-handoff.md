4. Branch Scope and Production Context
The Acting branch covers acting_scene and monologue behaviour inside TapeCoach’s current shared six-field production architecture. It addresses spoken acting, monologue, scene/duologue, reader-mediated scenes, screen/self-tape tasks, stage/classical/contemporary text handling, speech delivery, self-tape assessability, professional presentation boundaries, no-brief claim restraint, accessibility-safe language, methodology neutrality, physical/fight/comedy limitations and Acting-specific output specificity.
The revised branch outputs are intended to improve:
Acting-form specificity.
Speech-delivery label containment for spoken-only Acting / Monologue.
Behavioural evidence anchoring in all substantive Acting feedback.
Screen/self-tape camera and eyeline specificity.
Separation of technical assessability from acting quality.
Separation of speech audibility from speech performance.
Live-room-only and process-only claim boundaries.
Reader-as-process support handling.
No-brief role/world invention protection.
Accessibility-safe and anti-deficit feedback.
professional_presentation boundaries.
Timestamp and next-take specificity.
TapeCoach is live in production. The current live production system uses the shared pipeline and six operational score fields; the Acting branch refinements are completed research/rubric/report-behaviour proposals, not shipped production behaviour unless later implemented and rolled out. The live system’s Step 1 evidence pass and Step 2 polish pass are already production concepts, with Step 1 producing observation-only evidence and Step 2 using locked evidence without video; those current mechanics must remain preserved.
This branch must not change:
Score fields.
Acting / Monologue weights.
Backend or data flow.
Report schema or public JSON.
Caps, blockers, verdict thresholds or role-fit bounds.
Server-side recomputation.
Step 1 / Step 2 architecture.
Safety, material, presentation or accessibility scrubs.
Musical Theatre acting + song stabilised flow.

15. Compact Branch Handoff Pack
The Acting branch is mature enough for cross-branch consolidation and does not require an ACTING-PATCH. It is complete at research, synthesis, audit, revision, final-audit and output-specificity level, but its proposed refinements are not shipped production behaviour until later implementation and rollout.
The branch source base contains 43 source rows across official admissions / conservatoire sources, formal acting frameworks, screen/self-tape guidance, professional and union guidance, accessibility/fairness sources and specialist form-gap sources. The strongest evidence supports acting-form specificity, the distinction between tape-observable and live-room/process evidence, speech delivery as Acting evidence rather than singing, screen/self-tape task-specific camera handling, self-tape assessability over production polish, reader-as-process support, no-brief grounding, accessibility-safe non-deficit handling and behavioural evidence anchors. Comedy, physical/fight evidence, methodology-neutral positive descriptors, neurodivergence-specific operational detail and positive speech descriptors under access conditions remain limitations rather than patch blockers.
The audit produced ACTING-AUDIT-I01–I15 and ACTING-AUDIT-R01–R16. The revision implemented or conditionally contained these through ACTING-REV-C01–C16 and ACTING-REV-S01–S15. Final audit approved the revised baseline with explicit limitations under ACTING-FINAL-D01–D02. Output-specificity mapping then produced ACTING-OS-R01–R19, ACTING-OS-T01–T20 and ACTING-OS-I01–I10.
Implementation-readiness implications are clear: future production Acting outputs must be form-specific, evidence-led, speech-label-safe, access-safe, anti-polish, no-brief-safe, methodology-neutral and timestamp-specific while preserving the current six-field architecture, Acting weights, server-side recomputation, caps, blockers, verdict thresholds, role-fit bounds, report schema, Step 1 / Step 2 pipeline and MT acting + song anchor.
True Acting-only live-output QA has not been completed. The MT acting+song bridge QA provided useful partial evidence about component visibility, generic praise, timestamp underproduction, false specificity and presentation/polish drift, but it is not Acting release sign-off. Acting-only reports, comparison pages, JSON/report objects and exports are still required later to verify labels, renderer/export parity, no-brief handling, access-sensitive outputs and output enforcement.
