# Musical Theatre rubric research — discipline overview

> **Discipline thread overview** (part of [[drr-programme-overview]]). 17-step builds-on chain, originals dated **3–6 May 2026**, imported 2026-06-08. Concise entry point — the **full consolidated MT rubric** is the archived *Merged* file `knowledge/90-archive/discipline-rubric-research/1. MT/MT Rubric Research Merged.md` (not embedded here, by design).

## Arc

MT was the **first** discipline and became the template for the whole programme. It ran census/preflight → four extraction batches (B1 official admissions/conservatoire, B2 exam-board syllabi, B3 practitioner/self-tape, B4 subtype/style gap-fill) → synthesis → gap audit against the live slice → revision → final audit → a V5 maturity/output-spec stage with live-output QA → lessons → cross-branch handoff. The problem it solved: turning scattered official/exam/practitioner evidence into evidence-anchored MT requirements that kill generic feedback while staying inside a protected, already-stabilised scoring schema.

## What the consolidated rubric covers

- Acting-through-song with lyric/phrase/beat/objective evidence (not "good storytelling").
- Vocal technique **in service of** story and style (technique distinguished from communication; no "strong voice").
- Movement/dance only where visibly assessable (no generic "nice movement").
- Integration of acting + singing + movement (scene-to-song continuity; anti-silo).
- Style/subtype awareness — Golden Age vs contemporary, legit/mix/belt/pop-rock, jukebox (non-imitation), through-composed.
- Repertoire choice, training-potential vs casting-suitability, and self-tape assessability separated from performance quality.

## Deviations / findings

- MT is the **protected regression anchor**: guardrails forbade new score fields, schema/weight changes and importing exam-board percentages, so all gains were wording / evidence-requirement improvements inside the existing six fields (acting and vocal share one `vocal` field).
- It is the **messiest chain** (the pioneer): an early B2 draft ([[drr-mt-05-batch-b2-draft]]) was replaced by the final B2 ([[drr-mt-06-batch-b2]]), and the S0 census ([[drr-mt-01-source-census]]) was replaced by an S0 v1.1 "repair" ([[drr-mt-02-source-census-repair]]) that swapped fragile citation markers for stable source IDs.
- Through-composed MT stayed an explicit unresolved evidence gap. The final handoff records that live outputs **fail** V5 output-specificity QA, so production sign-off is blocked (research is not).

## Key lesson

Preserve an unbroken traceability chain — Source ID → evidence finding → audit recommendation → revision change → final-audit check — to prevent auditing the wrong rubric version, making unsupported changes, or losing evidence provenance.
