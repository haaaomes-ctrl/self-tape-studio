12. Acting Patch Decision
Decision: No Acting patch required; branch is mature enough for implementation planning with limitations.
Rationale:
Acting has completed all research/rubric stages through Output Specificity / Non-Regression Test Mapping.
Source coverage is broad and statused: 43 rows across B1–B4, with inaccessible, partial, process-only and course-shaped limitations explicitly carried.
ACTING-SYN-F01–F17, ACTING-AUDIT-I01–I15, ACTING-AUDIT-R01–R16, ACTING-REV-C01–C16, ACTING-REV-S01–S15 and ACTING-OS-R/T IDs form a complete traceability chain.
The final audit already concluded there were no critical blockers requiring a return to ACTING-REV.
The remaining weaknesses are known limitations rather than evidence failures: live-output absence, label/render verification, comedy thinness, physical/fight source shape, methodology-neutral descriptor range and broad neurodivergence operational detail.
Under lean v5, incomplete live-output QA is not a reason to repeat research automatically; it is a later QA and release-sign-off gate.
Critical blockers: none.
Patch scope if needed: not applicable.
What should not be reopened:
Full Acting research.
ACTING-SYN, ACTING-AUDIT, ACTING-REV or ACTING-FINAL-AUDIT.
Source extraction, except narrow provenance verification if future implementation planning specifically needs it.
Comedy, physical/fight or neurodivergence research unless the product later needs a first-class subtype or access-specific branch.
Deep Research required: no.
Standard ChatGPT sufficient: yes, for future implementation-readiness consolidation and live QA planning.
Live outputs required for patch: no patch is required. Live outputs are required later for release sign-off.
Can proceed before implementation planning: yes. Acting may move into cross-branch implementation planning with explicit limitations.
