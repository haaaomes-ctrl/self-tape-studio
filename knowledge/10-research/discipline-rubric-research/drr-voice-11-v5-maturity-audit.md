14. Reusable Handoff Pack
VOICE-V5-MATURITY-AUDIT reviewed the completed Voice / Singing branch against the newer lean v5 branch-development standard. The maturity decision is that no Voice / Singing patch is required. The branch is mature enough for future implementation planning with explicit limitations and later live-output QA.
The strongest Voice / Singing assets are its full traceability spine and mature safety/fairness boundaries. The branch preserves VOICE-S001–VOICE-S052, VOICE-SYN-F01–F17, VOICE-AUDIT-I01–I20, VOICE-AUDIT-R01–R21, VOICE-REV-C01–C17, VOICE-REV-S01–S18, VOICE-FINAL-D01, VOICE-OS-R onward, VOICE-OS-T01–T31 and VOICE-OS-I01–I12. It clarifies vocal as sung-vocal evidence in Song / Musical Theatre while preserving Acting speech-delivery and Dance no-singing containment. It keeps Song vocal-centred but requires interpretive evidence where assessable: lyric intention, communication, phrase action, story and acting-through-song. It converts formal frameworks into observable descriptor language for pitch, rhythm, tone, diction, phrasing, communication and interpretation without importing marks, grades or weights.
The branch also strongly separates audio/technical assessability from vocal merit, treats accompaniment/backing/self-accompaniment as context-specific, blocks production polish and paid-resource access as talent evidence, and protects fixed-material policy. Safety and fairness coverage is mature: no vocal-health diagnosis from tape; strain-like, fatigued or hoarse sound remains observation-only; access needs, disability, neurodivergence, hearing difference, accent, speech difference, gender-diverse voice and assistive technology are non-deficit context.
Limitations remain but are correctly carried forward: detailed belt/mix/registration evidence is thin; jazz/folk/broad commercial-pop descriptors are uneven; full actor-musician evidence is partial; neurodivergence operational detail is broad; no true Voice-only live outputs exist; frontend labels, comparison-page parity, rendered timestamp parity, score stability, implementation behaviour and rollout effects remain unverified.
The branch does not need a focused patch. Later work should be implementation planning plus live-output QA when Voice / Singing outputs exist. Live-output QA is not required to prove research maturity, but it is required before release confidence for implemented revised behaviour.
