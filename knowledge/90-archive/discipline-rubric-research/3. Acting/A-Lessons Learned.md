\f1\b0\fs24 \cf0 Select 
\f0\b Voice / Singing
\f1\b0  as the next discipline branch.\
It is the highest-leverage next branch because the 
\f2\fs26 vocal
\f1\fs24  field is the most overloaded shared field in the current TapeCoach architecture: it means sung vocal for Song/MT, speech delivery for Acting/Monologue, and movement-technique proxy for Dance. That creates label, weighting and report-interpretation risk across every completed branch.\
Voice/Singing is also explicitly underdeveloped in the current baseline. The existing control sheet says Song/Voice already covers vocal performance, musical interpretation, acting/storytelling through song, audio clarity and brief adherence, but still lacks style-specific criteria for classical/legit, contemporary MT, belt, mix, pop/rock, jazz and folk, plus vocal-health/sustainability language, diction, resonance, passaggio, support, vibrato and stylistic authenticity.\
This also protects the MT anchor. Musical Theatre is currently the stabilised flow and must continue to show both Acting and Vocal where acting and singing are present; the current baseline also notes that MT acting-through-song is still implicit and that song feedback can over-focus on vocal technique rather than lyric/storytelling.\
